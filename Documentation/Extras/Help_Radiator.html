Radiator

Block Inputs

Tin - Temperature of cooling fluid, degF (1x1)
W - Mass flow rate of cooling fluid, lbm/sec (1x1)
T(t-1) - Temperature of cooling fluid delayed by one time step, degF (1x1)

Block Outputs

Tout - Temperature of cooling fluid after radiation, degF (1x1)
Outputs - Bus of Block Inner Variables
Prad - Radition power, W (1x1)
Trad - Temperature decrease due to radiation, degF (1x1)
Tout - Temperature of cooling fluid after radiation, degF (1x1)
Tin - Temperature of cooling fluid, degF (1x1)
Tamb or T0 - Ambient temperature, degF (1x1)
W - Mass flow rate of cooling fluid, lbm/sec (1x1)
T(t) - Temperature of cooling fluid to be delayed by one time step, degF (1x1)

Block Mask

T0 - Ambient Temperature, degF (1x1)
A - Area of radiator, degF (1x1)
eps - Emissivity, unitless (0-1) (1x1)
cp - Cooling fluid specific heat capacity, BTU/(lbm-degF) (1x1)

Block Calculations

General Variables
