Heat Exchanger

Block Inputs

TH_In - Input temperature of hot fluid, degF (1x1)
TC_In - Input temperature of cold fluid, degF (1x1)
WH - Mass flow rate of hot fluid, lbm/sec (1x1)
WC - Mass flow rate of cold fluid, lbm/sec (1x1)
TH_In(t-1) - Temperature of hot fluid delayed by one time step, degF (1x1)

Block Outputs

TH_Out - Output temperature of hot fluid, degF (1x1)
TC_Out - Output temperature of cold fluid, degF (1x1)
Outputs - Bus of Block Inner Variables
TC_In - Input temperature of cold fluid, degF (1x1)
TH_In - Input temperature of hot fluid, degF (1x1)
TC_Out - Output temperature of cold fluid, degF (1x1)
TH_Out - Output temperature of hot fluid, degF (1x1)
dTC - change in cold fluid temperature, degF (1x1)
dTH - change in hot fluid temperature, degF (1x1)
WC - Mass flow rate of cold fluid, lbm/sec (1x1)
WH - Mass flow rate of hot fluid, lbm/sec (1x1)
Qdot - Heat rate of change, J/s (1x1)
Cmin - Minimum specific heat capacity, BTU/(lbm-degF)
Eff - Effectiveness, unitless (0-1) (1x1)
TH_In(t) - Temperature of hot fluid to be delayed by one time step, degF (1x1)

Block Mask

cp_h - Specific heat capacity of hot fluid, BTU/(lbm-degF) (1x1)
cp_c - Specific heat capacity of cold fluid, BTU/(lbm-degF) (1x1)
Wh_vec - Hot fluid mass flow rate breakpoints for effectivness table, lbm/sec (1xn)
Wc_vec - Cold fluid mass flow rate breakpoints for effectivness table, lbm/sec (1xm)
eff_vec - Effectiveness table values, unitless (0-1) (nxm)

Block Calculations

General Variables

t - time, sec
n - number of hot fluid mass flow rate breakpoints, unitless
n - number of cold fluid mass flow rate breakpoints, unitless
